# Music — drop your songs here

Put each song in this folder (`Godot/Music/`) with **exactly** the file name below.
`.ogg`, `.mp3` or `.wav` all work (if two exist, .ogg wins, then .mp3, then .wav).
Songs loop forever, so a track that ends where it began sounds best. A missing song falls back to the old built-in track.
No Godot import is needed: just restart the game (Play.bat).

| File name | When it plays |
|---|---|
| `title` | Title screen, menus, character select, ending |
| `stage1_road` / `stage1_boss` | Stage 1 星光參道 / boss 天狼 |
| `stage2_road` / `stage2_boss` | Stage 2 四時繪卷 / boss 千秋 |
| `stage3_road` / `stage3_boss` | Stage 3 雲上之路 / boss 嵐 |
| `stage4_road` / `stage4_boss` | Stage 4 四君子庭園 / boss 墨竹 |
| `stage5_road` / `stage5_boss` | Stage 5 五行仙壇 / boss 玄 |
| `stage6_road` / `stage6_boss` | Stage 6 天宮 / final boss 燁 |
| `extra_road` / `extra_boss` | Extra 雀莊・天和閣 (玄 the midboss keeps the road song) / boss 一索 |
| `extraif_road` / `extraif_boss` | Extra-IF 崩落天宮・四凶之淵 (the road beast keeps the road song) / the four perils' relay |

Example: `Godot/Music/stage3_boss.mp3`

# Theme prompts

Written for a music generator (Suno, Udio, …). All are instrumental, made to loop, in the spirit of Touhou-style
bullet-hell music: a strong lead melody over driving rhythm, East Asian colour, about 2–3 minutes.
Road themes are lighter and travel forward; boss themes are faster, denser and more dramatic, with a hook you remember.

**title — 星墜彈幕**
Instrumental, loopable. Night sky over a mountain shrine, stars beginning to fall. Slow-building intro of koto and
celesta, then a hopeful, heroic main theme on erhu and strings over soft taiko and piano. Wistful but determined,
the feeling of a long journey about to start. 100 BPM.

**stage1_road — 星光參道 (Starlit Shrine Path)**
Instrumental, loopable, upbeat. Climbing stone steps to a shrine at night under a sky full of stars. Bright piano
arpeggios and glockenspiel sparkle over a light drum groove, shakuhachi melody, plucked koto. Fresh, curious,
first-stage energy. 145 BPM.

**stage1_boss — 天狼 (Tenrou, star-chanting onmyoji)**
Instrumental, loopable, Touhou-style boss theme. A calm onmyoji who reads fate in the stars. Mysterious minor-key
melody on shinobue and synth lead, shimmering bell textures like constellations, steady four-on-the-floor kick,
rising string runs. Elegant, cool, slightly ominous. 160 BPM.

**stage2_road — 四時繪卷 (Scroll of the Four Seasons)**
Instrumental, loopable. Walking through a painted scroll where spring blossoms, summer sun, autumn leaves and winter
snow flow past. Flowing pipa and flute melody, warm strings, gentle swing in the drums, each section shifting colour
like a new season. Graceful and lively. 140 BPM.

**stage2_boss — 千秋 (Chiaki, painter of the four seasons)**
Instrumental, loopable, Touhou-style boss theme. An elegant painter whose brush strokes become danmaku. Sweeping
guzheng glissandos and erhu lead over driving drums and staccato strings; a bright, singable hook; mid-section
modulates as if turning to the next season. Beautiful but fierce. 165 BPM.

**stage3_road — 雲上之路 (Road Above the Clouds)**
Instrumental, loopable. Flying over a sea of clouds, wind and open sky, a storm gathering on the horizon. Airy
synth pads, soaring flute, fast hi-hats, a gust-like whoosh between phrases, building tension toward the end. 150 BPM.

**stage3_boss — 嵐 (Arashi, master of weather)**
Instrumental, loopable, Touhou-style boss theme. Sun, rain, thunder and fog commanded by one man. Stormy and wild:
pounding taiko, distorted guitar and erhu trading the melody, lightning-crack synth stabs, rain-like piano runs.
High energy, dramatic key changes. 175 BPM.

**stage4_road — 四君子庭園 (Garden of the Four Gentlemen)**
Instrumental, loopable. A scholar's moonlit garden of plum, orchid, bamboo and chrysanthemum. Refined guqin and
dizi melody, ink-wash calm on top, a steady pulse underneath that slowly grows tense. Literati, poetic, quietly
dangerous. 135 BPM.

**stage4_boss — 墨竹 (Mochiku, scholar of the four gentlemen)**
Instrumental, loopable, Touhou-style boss theme. A proud scholar whose calligraphy cuts like a blade. Sharp
guqin/pipa plucks, rapid bamboo-flute lead, crisp snare rolls, pizzicato strings, a noble melody that turns
aggressive in the second half. Disciplined and intense. 170 BPM.

**stage5_road — 五行仙壇 (Altar of the Five Elements)**
Instrumental, loopable. Ascending a Taoist altar where wood, fire, earth, metal and water swirl around. Ritual bells
and gongs, mystic synth arpeggios, deep drums, a melody that changes instrument for each element. Solemn and
grand, building to the summit. 150 BPM.

**stage5_boss — 玄 (Gen, sorcerer of the five elements)**
Instrumental, loopable, Touhou-style boss theme. A powerful, serious sorcerer guarding a secret. Dark minor key,
heavy taiko and orchestral hits, suona and erhu lead, choir pads, cycling motif that passes through five
instrument colours. Epic and tense. 175 BPM.

**stage6_road — 天宮 (The Heavenly Palace)**
Instrumental, loopable. The gates of the celestial palace, golden halls above the sky, the end of the journey.
Majestic brass and strings, temple bells, a noble melody that feels sacred and a little sad, steady march-like
drums. Short and solemn, final-stage atmosphere. 140 BPM.

**stage6_boss — 燁 (Yō, guardian of the four symbols — final boss)**
Instrumental, loopable, Touhou-style final boss theme. A solemn guardian channelling the azure dragon, white tiger,
vermilion bird and black tortoise. Full orchestra plus rock band, erhu and shakuhachi leads, choir, a heroic yet
tragic main hook, four contrasting sections for the four beasts, huge climax. 180 BPM.

**extra_road — 雀莊・天和閣 (Mahjong Parlour: Tenhou Pavilion)**
Instrumental, loopable. A glamorous, slightly decadent mahjong pavilion in the sky, tiles clacking, lanterns glowing.
Jazzy Chinese swing: upright bass, brushed drums, piano, erhu and muted trumpet, playful and sly, gradually
picking up speed. 155 BPM.

**extra_boss — 一索 (Isso, Mahjong Saint of the Yakuman)**
Instrumental, loopable, Touhou-style extra boss theme. A laughing gambler who never loses. Fast, flashy and cocky:
electro-swing meets Chinese orchestra, slap bass, brass stabs, rapid pipa runs, a catchy show-off hook, sudden
breaks like a winning hand being revealed. Very high energy. 185 BPM.

**extraif_road — 崩落天宮・四凶之淵 (Collapsed Heavenly Palace: Abyss of the Four Perils)**
Instrumental, loopable. The heavenly palace in ruins, red cracks across a dark sky, ancient beasts stirring.
Ominous and heavy: low drones, distorted taiko, eerie suona, broken temple bells, a desperate melody pushing forward.
Dark and tense. 150 BPM.

**extraif_boss — 四凶 (The Four Perils: Taotie, Taowu, Hundun, Qiongqi)**
Instrumental, loopable, Touhou-style extra boss theme. Four primordial monsters fighting in relay, then all at once.
Monstrous and epic: symphonic metal with Chinese instruments, pounding double-kick drums, roaring brass, a
savage erhu/suona lead, four short motifs (greedy, stubborn, chaotic, winged) that combine into one massive final
section. 190 BPM.
